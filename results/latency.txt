WHAT THIS SHOWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Processing cost is measured and is a rounding error against the window.
Latency in the current architecture is dominated by WHEN THE JOB RUNS,
not by how long the work takes. Shortening the window is the lever;
making the processing faster is not.

The <5s target is unreachable with any batch window above ~5 seconds,
regardless of how fast the processing gets. That is the argument for
streaming, and it does not depend on any claim about a broker.

WHAT THIS IS NOT: not a benchmark of Kafka, Kinesis, Flink or any
broker. No network, no cluster, no queue, 25 events. Evidence Tier 3
(benchmark run, method stated), not Tier 4.
